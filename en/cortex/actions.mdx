**Actions** is the calendar of what the **humans** did to move the objectives: raise a price, switch a supplier, change a creative, renegotiate a contract. Each action is tied to an area (and, where it makes sense, to an objective/KPI). The big value: with one click, the AI **assesses the impact** of each action in the data — and tells you whether it **improved, worsened or didn't change** things.

## How it works, in one sentence

A human action is recorded (by you or coming from a meeting) → you click **Analyze impact** → the AI investigates the data around the action's date, compares the KPI before/after and assigns a **verdict** with a justification → this feeds the [Learnings](/en/cortex/learnings).

## The calendar

Just like a calendar, with **Week, Month, Quarter and Year** views and navigation via arrows + **Today**. Actions appear colored by **area**, grouped by day. A per-area summary shows how many actions are positive, negative, neutral, and so on.

### Where actions come from

## Analyze impact — the part that makes the difference

This is the heart of the screen. There are two ways to trigger it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="A specific action">
    Click an action to open the details panel and use **Analyze impact of this action**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="In bulk (the whole period)">
    The **Analyze impact of actions** button opens a confirmation and assesses all the actions in the selected **period/areas** at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Asynchronous and in real time

The analysis **doesn't freeze the screen**. As soon as you trigger it, the action shows an **"Analyzing…"** state and the AI works in the background — investigating the data, comparing the KPI before and after the action's date. **The result appears on its own, in seconds**, without you having to reload. When it's ready, the card shows the **verdict** and, on opening it, the **justification** with numbers.

<Info>
Actions coming from **meetings** (or at company level, with no owning agent) are analyzed by the native **Cortex** — the company's management agent. You don't need to configure anything: clicking "Analyze impact" just works.
</Info>

### The verdicts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Positive / Negative / Neutral" icon="scale-balanced">
    There's evidence that the action **improved** (positive), **worsened** (negative) or **didn't change** (neutral) the KPI. These three generate a [Learning](/en/cortex/learnings).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Too early" icon="hourglass-start">
    *"too_early"*: the KPI is measurable, but not enough time has passed yet (or there's no post-action measurement) to assess it. E.g. a renegotiation made today doesn't yet show in CAC.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cannot assess" icon="ban">
    *"cannot_assess"*: there's no measurable KPI or available data to judge it. A sign that a data source still needs connecting or a KPI needs tying to the action.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
The AI is **conservative**: it only marks positive/negative when there's real evidence in the data. When in doubt, it prefers "too early" over inventing an effect. This keeps the learnings trustworthy.
</Tip>

## Create, edit and delete actions

Besides **New action**, you edit or delete from the action's details panel. Every action can be linked to an **agent → objective → KPI** so the analysis knows exactly which number to look at.

## Permissions

Analyzing the actions of an **agent** requires configuration permission on that agent; analyzing the actions of the **whole company** (general bulk run) requires owner/admin.
