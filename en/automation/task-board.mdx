The **Task Board** organizes work visually (To do → Doing → Done). The differentiator: **people and agents share the same board**. AI tasks **run on their own** at the time you set; **human** tasks come with acceptance, deadlines, action checklists and evidence-backed completion — and **everything is recorded** in an auditable history.

## The lanes

| Lane | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Backlog** | Ideas and pending items without an owner or a moment yet. |
| **Scheduled** | Tasks that kick in at a future time (recurring or dated). |
| **To do** | Ready to be executed (by a person or an agent). |
| **Running** | Executing right now. |
| **For Approval** | Waiting on a human decision — execution approval or the **acceptance** of an assigned task. |
| **Done** | Finished. Recurring tasks automatically spawn the next occurrence. |

## Every task type has its own look

Glance at the board and know who handles what:

- **👤 Person** — blue border and an avatar with the assignee's initials.
- **🤖 AI** — purple border, executed by an agent or team.
- **🌐 API** — orange border, calls an external system (webhook).

## Creating a task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Automation → Task Board and click “New task”" />
  <Step title="Write the description — with real formatting">
    The description field is a **rich, document-style editor**: bold, headings, lists, links — and **pasting from outside keeps the styling**. A good description is what the person (or the agent) will read to do the work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who executes">
    - **Agent / Team:** the AI runs it when the task kicks in.
    - **Person:** type the name — **autocomplete** lists company members. The person receives an **acceptance request**.
    - **Webhook/API:** triggers an external system (advanced).
  </Step>
  <Step title="(For people) Set the “Due by”">
    The delivery deadline shows on the card, turns **red when it expires** and amber when it's close — and feeds the "Expiring" and "Overdue" filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="(Optional) Recurrence, visibility, initiative and sub-task">
    Tasks can repeat on a schedule, be **public, private or area-scoped**, belong to a roadmap **initiative** and be born as a **sub-task** of another.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Human tasks — acceptance, delivery and evidence

When you assign a task to a **person**, it doesn't just land on their pile:

1. **Acceptance.** The person gets notified (bell + email) and decides: **accept** (the task moves to To do) or **decline** — declining **requires a reason**, which goes back to whoever asked. Only the assignee can decide; not even the company owner can accept on someone else's behalf.
2. **Actions.** Inside the task lives an **action checklist** — the steps of the work. Every checked item records who did it and when.
3. **Completion with proof.** Completing requires telling **how it was done** and having **at least one action recorded** (you can type them right there). Attach **evidence** (spreadsheet, screenshot, contract). The result becomes a complete block — note + steps + evidence + delivery time — in the same place where AI writes its answers.

<Info>
**Self-assignment without bureaucracy:** a task you create **for yourself** is born accepted and goes straight to the chosen lane — acceptance exists to protect people receiving work *from someone else*.
</Info>

<Info>
**Recurring human tasks:** every new occurrence asks for acceptance again — accepting January's report is not accepting February's. (If the recurrence is yours, with yourself, it's born accepted.)
</Info>

## Sub-tasks and Roadmap

Break big work into **sub-tasks** (up to 4 levels) — each can go to a **person or an agent**, mixed in the same tree. The **parent's progress adds up on its own** as children finish.

On the **Roadmap** tab, create **initiatives** (the layer above: "Launch the new portal by September") and tie tasks to them. The initiative shows a real progress bar computed from actual work.

## Visibility — who sees what

| Mode | Who can see it |
|---|---|
| **Public** (default) | Everyone in the company — the behavior it's always had. |
| **Private** | Only the creator, the assignee and watchers. |
| **Area** | Every member of the chosen area. |

Owners and admins always see everything (governance). The filter is enforced server-side: non-participants can't see the task even with a direct link.

## Filters and saved Views

- **Quick filters**: Mine × All · 👤 People / 🤖 AI / 🌐 API · ⏳ Expiring / 🔴 Overdue — resolved server-side, across all lanes.
- **Views**: save a named filter ("My criticals: high priority + mine + overdue") and apply it with one click.

## Dashboard — your board's metrics

The **Dashboard** tab works like the [Pipes dashboard](/en/automation/pipes): pick **what to measure** (lead time, cycle time, weekly throughput, load per person, awaiting acceptance, time to accept, declined, overdue with targets…), **how to break it down** (person, project, initiative, priority, week…) and the chart type. Drag, resize and save the layout.

The dashboard **obeys the active filter**: filter "Mine + People" and the charts compute over exactly that slice — with a badge showing the applied filter.

## History — everything is recorded

Every task carries its own **auditable history**: who created it, who assigned it, who accepted or declined (and why), every move between lanes (from → to), **every edit with the changed fields**, every checked action, AI executions (success/failure) and the completion. The record is immutable — editing a task never rewrites its past.

## Your Personal Assistant knows your tasks

- In the workspace, the **“My work”** card highlights what awaits **your acceptance** — accept/decline right there, and clicking any task opens the **full modal** (actions, completion, sub-tasks, history) **without leaving the screen**.
- By conversation, the assistant **lists, details, accepts, declines, completes and checks actions** on your tasks — and can **retrieve any task by name**, even completed or archived, always with a direct link.
- The [Cortex](/en/cortex/overview) sees the whole board within each person's clearance: overdue, awaiting acceptance, declines with reasons, load per person and recent completions with the how-it-was-done note.

## Recurrence — run on its own

When marking a task as recurring, pick the interval:

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| **Every X minutes** | Near-real-time monitoring (e.g. every 1 min). |
| **Every X hours** | Periodic checks (e.g. every 2h). |
| **Daily** | A summary every morning at 8am. |
| **Weekly** | A report every Monday. |
| **Monthly** | Closing on the 1st. |

Also set the **time** and the **timezone** (from the company [Settings](/en/administration/settings)). On every completion, the **next occurrence is created automatically**.

<Info>
**Reliability:** recurring tasks fire on time and **survive server restarts** — they resume on their own.
</Info>

## Assigning to an agent

1. On the card, under **Execute with**, pick **Agent** and choose one.
2. Make sure the agent has access to the data/tools the task requires.
3. When the task kicks in, the agent executes and records the result on the card.

<Info>
A **person's** task is never executed by AI — even if dragged to "To do". The work is human; AI only helps track it.
</Info>

## Task, process or team?

| Use… | When |
|---|---|
| **Task** | One-off work or a simple **single-step** routine — human or agent. |
| [Process](/en/automation/processes) | **Multiple chained steps** that repeat, with forms, approvers and rules. |
| [Team](/en/intelligence/agent-teams) | You need **several AI specialties** collaborating. |

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I assigned a task and it vanished from the person's board">
    It's in **For Approval**, awaiting acceptance. The person also sees it in their workspace ("My work" card) and notifications.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't complete a human task">
    Only **the assignee** completes it — and it requires telling how it was done plus **at least one recorded action** (you can type them at completion time).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A recurring task didn't fire on time">
    Check that it's **active** and the Settings **timezone** is right. Open the card's history for the last run.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The assigned agent didn't execute">
    Confirm the agent has **access to the required data/tools** and that you have **use** level on it. Try the same request in chat.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I want to pause a routine without deleting it">
    Disable the task (active toggle). It stops spawning occurrences and can be re-enabled later.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
